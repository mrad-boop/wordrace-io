👋 Welcome to WordRace.io
What is WordRace?

WordRace.io is a competitive vocabulary game inspired by the classic "Petit Bac" (Categories) format. Players race against the clock to fill in words starting with a randomly drawn letter across 8 different categories — all in just 40 seconds.

WordRace offers two distinct zones: a completely Free Zone for casual play against AI bots, and a Play-to-Earn Zone where players wager real USDT and compete for cash prizes on Polygon and Solana blockchains.

🎯No registration required for the Free Zone. Just click "Play Now" and you're in within seconds.
🎮 How to Play
The basics of every round
1
A random letter is drawn
At the start of each round, a letter is randomly selected (e.g. "M"). All your answers must begin with this letter.
2
40-second Blitz Timer starts
You have exactly 40 seconds to fill in as many of the 8 categories as possible. Speed and vocabulary are both rewarded.
3
Type your answers
On desktop, use Tab to move between categories. On mobile, swipe between category cards. All inputs enforce the correct first letter automatically.
4
Validation happens instantly
Each word is validated in real-time using our 3-tier engine: exact dictionary match, fuzzy correction, and AI arbitration as fallback.
5
Results & scores revealed
After the round, scores are shown per category. Unique words (only you answered with that word) earn bonus points.

✅ Word Validation Engine
3-tier system · Target response time under 500ms

WordRace uses a proprietary 3-tier validation engine to ensure fast, fair and accurate word checking:

T1
🔍 Exact Dictionary Match
Word is checked against our categorised local dictionary (~600+ words per category). Response time: ~80ms. If matched, word is instantly validated.
T2
🔧 Fuzzy Match (Levenshtein Distance)
Tolerates typos based on word length — 0 typos for 3–5 letter words, 1 for 6–8 letters, 2 for 9+ letters. Automatically corrects to the closest valid word. Response time: ~200ms.
T3
🤖 AI Arbitrator (Fallback)
For slang, rare names, or words not in our dictionary. A Claude AI model evaluates the word and its category fit. Result is cached to avoid future API costs. Response time: ~400ms.
✏️When a word is auto-corrected by Tier 2, you receive full points. The corrected word is shown in your results.
⚔️ Duel Arena
Real-time multiplayer wagering — Smart contract escrow

The Duel Arena allows 2 to 8 players to compete in real-time vocabulary rounds with a USDT wager. All funds are locked in a Polygon smart contract immediately upon room creation and released automatically to the winner at game end.

How It Works

1
Create or join a room
Set your wager ($1–$50 USDT), number of players (2–8), and rounds (1, 3, or 5). Share the Room ID with opponents.
2
Funds locked in escrow
Each player's wager is deducted immediately and locked in a verified Polygon smart contract. WordRace never holds funds directly.
3
Play — 40s rounds
Fill 8 categories starting with the round's letter. Tab to navigate on desktop, swipe on mobile. Opponent's answers are hidden during play.
4
Automatic payout
The player with the highest score wins 95% of the prize pool. The 5% rake is retained by WordRace. Payout is triggered automatically by the smart contract.

🛡️ Fair Play Rules & Anti-Cheat
WordRace operates a zero-tolerance policy against cheating

Prohibited Actions

  • Tab switching: Leaving the browser tab or switching apps during a round is detected instantly. First offence = warning. Second offence = round forfeited (score = 0).
  • Copy & Paste: All text inputs block paste events. Attempting to paste is logged and may trigger review.
  • Automated scripts / macros: Keystroke timing is analysed in real-time. Typing speeds inconsistent with human behaviour result in immediate disqualification.
  • VPN / IP manipulation: Using VPNs to bypass regional restrictions or create multiple accounts is prohibited.
  • Multiple accounts: Each player is permitted one account. Duplicate accounts are permanently banned.
🚫Players found cheating in paid modes (Daily League, Duel Arena) will be permanently banned and all associated funds may be forfeited. There is no appeal process for bot-detected disqualifications.
